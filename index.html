Zheti qater zhane zheti kuij

Añyz bojynsha, erte zamanda bir qarijanyñ zheti uly zhut zhyly ar-turli sebeptermen ajal qushady. Әrbir uly qaza bolghan sajyn qaigyly ake bir tishten asyq qojyp, sypyryp tartqan tindi shertip, kuij shygharghan eken.

Osylyajsha xalyq arasyna «Zhetigen» atauly zheti tindi, mungi men taghzymyn zhetkizetin kieli aspap taraghan.

Zhetigen qurylymy

Zhetigen — qushtauly aghashtan oylyp zhasalatyn, tinderinin astyna asyq qojylatyn koñildi aspap. Qazirgi tanda tinder sany 15-23-ke dejin zhetip, diapazony keneygen.
